Frequently asked questions

Clear answers about thumbnail quality, tools, and privacy.

Can every YouTube thumbnail be downloaded in 4K?

No. YouTube provides thumbnail images at source-dependent sizes. This tool previews the selected available endpoint; it does not turn a small source image into genuine 4K.

Which image formats are supported?

The browser workspace accepts common JPG, PNG, and WEBP images. Conversion support depends on the browser.

Are the AI tools fully connected?

Advanced analysis, true super-resolution, and background removal need external AI services. They are marked Integration required until those services are connected.

Is my uploaded image sent to a server?

The resize and format conversion workspace processes images locally in your browser. No upload backend is configured in this starter website.

Does this website guarantee YouTube views or CTR?

No. Any design score or appeal estimate is not a real YouTube CTR prediction and cannot guarantee views.
